What We Do

Our Six Pillars of Change

Every initiative we run is rooted in community need and guided by the belief that empowerment begins at the grassroots.

📚

Education

Scholarships, coaching centres, and digital literacy programs for underprivileged children and youth.

👩

Women's Development

Skill-building, self-help groups, and entrepreneurship training for financial independence.

🏕

Tribal Welfare

Healthcare outreach, cultural preservation, and livelihood support for tribal communities.

🧓

Elderly Care

Regular health check-ups, community companionship, and assistance for senior citizens.

🥗

Nutrition

Mid-day meal drives and anemia prevention programs for children and adolescent girls.

🌱

Sustainable Livelihoods

Organic farming support, farmer cooperatives, and micro-enterprise funding.

Our Story

Rooted in Seva, Driven by Purpose

Dhaathree Foundation was born from a simple but powerful conviction: every person deserves the chance to flourish. Founded in 2019 and registered under Regd. No. 103/2019, we began our journey in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh.

The word Dhaathree (ధాత్రి) means Earth — the sustainer of all life. Like the earth, we strive to nourish without discrimination.
