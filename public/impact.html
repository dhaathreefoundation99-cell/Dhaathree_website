Where Your Support Goes

  • 1,025 Children

    Enrolled across 23 Akshara Dhaathree after-school study centres.

  • Women’s Livelihoods

    Trained in livelihood skills and supported through self-help groups.

  • Tribal Communities

    Reached with health awareness outreach and livelihood support.

  • 50 Elders

    Cared for at Ananda Nilayam, our day care centre for the aged, with regular health check-ups and companionship.

  • Child Nutrition

    Children benefiting from nutrition and anemia-prevention programs.

  • Farmers

    Supported through cooperatives and sustainable farming initiatives.

Working in Alignment with National Programmes

Our work complements and supports the following Government of India initiatives.

Education

Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan, National Education Policy 2020

Through our Akshara Dhaathree after-school study centres

Nutrition

POSHAN Abhiyan, Anaemia Mukt Bharat

Through nutritional support for children, pregnant women and the elderly

Livelihoods

Skill India Mission

Through vocational training in tailoring, embroidery, handicrafts and computer literacy

Women

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, National Rural Livelihoods Mission

Through Mahila Dhaathree self-help groups and rights awareness training

Elder Care

Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007

Through Ananda Nilayam day care for the aged
