Our Projects

Ten programme wings of grassroots work, each designed to meet a community's most urgent needs.

Dhaathree Wings

Ten dedicated wings, each carrying forward a distinct mission under the Dhaathree Foundation umbrella.

  • Akshara DhaathreeEducation & Literacy WingSpreading the light of education among children through learning support, mentoring, and skill development — with special focus on underprivileged students.
  • Ananda NilayamDay Care Centre for the Aged WingProviding a safe, loving, and dignified shelter for the elderly, homeless, and vulnerable. Ananda Nilayam ensures access to proper nutrition, healthcare, and community care in a family environment.
  • Aahara DhaathreeFood Support WingProviding food support and distribution programs to needy communities, reducing hunger and promoting food security for children, elders, and families.
  • Arogya DhaathreeHealth Awareness & Community Wellbeing WingImproving community health and well-being through awareness programs, medical camps, and accessible healthcare for the vulnerable.
  • Vidya DhaathreeKnowledge & Skill WingPromoting learning beyond the classroom — workshops, skill-building, and practical training that help youth discover their abilities.
  • Vikasa DhaathreeDevelopment WingDriving overall community development — social, educational, and economic growth through inclusive, sustainable programs.
  • Vana DhaathreeEnvironment WingProtecting nature through tree plantation drives, conservation awareness, and eco-friendly practices for a greener future.
  • Mahila DhaathreeWomen Empowerment WingEmpowering women through education, skills, and leadership programs that build confidence, dignity, and self-reliance.
  • Sramika DhaathreeWorkers Support WingSupporting workers’ welfare and dignity through rights awareness, skill development, and opportunities for labour communities.
  • General ServicesCommunity Support WingResponding to diverse community needs with welfare, awareness, and volunteer-driven initiatives across every area of public welfare.
