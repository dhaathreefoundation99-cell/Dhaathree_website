Our Projects

Ten programme wings of grassroots work, each designed to meet a community's most urgent needs.

Dhaathree Wings

Ten dedicated wings, each carrying forward a distinct mission under the Dhaathree Foundation umbrella.

Akshara Dhaathree

Education & Literacy Wing

Spreading the light of education among children through learning support, mentoring, and skill development — with special focus on underprivileged students.

Learn MoreDonate

Ananda Nilayam

Day Care Centre for the Aged Wing

Providing a safe, loving, and dignified shelter for the elderly, homeless, and vulnerable. Ananda Nilayam ensures access to proper nutrition, healthcare, and community care in a family environment.

Learn MoreDonate

Aahara Dhaathree

Food Support Wing

Providing food support and distribution programs to needy communities, reducing hunger and promoting food security for children, elders, and families.

Learn MoreDonate

Arogya Dhaathree

Health Awareness & Community Wellbeing Wing

Improving community health and well-being through awareness programs, medical camps, and accessible healthcare for the vulnerable.

Learn MoreDonate

Vidya Dhaathree

Knowledge & Skill Wing

Promoting learning beyond the classroom — workshops, skill-building, and practical training that help youth discover their abilities.

Learn MoreDonate

Vikasa Dhaathree

Development Wing

Driving overall community development — social, educational, and economic growth through inclusive, sustainable programs.

Learn MoreDonate

Vana Dhaathree

Environment Wing

Protecting nature through tree plantation drives, conservation awareness, and eco-friendly practices for a greener future.

Learn MoreDonate

Mahila Dhaathree

Women Empowerment Wing

Empowering women through education, skills, and leadership programs that build confidence, dignity, and self-reliance.

Learn MoreDonate

Sramika Dhaathree

Workers Support Wing

Supporting workers’ welfare and dignity through rights awareness, skill development, and opportunities for labour communities.

Learn MoreDonate

General Services

Community Support Wing

Responding to diverse community needs with welfare, awareness, and volunteer-driven initiatives across every area of public welfare.

Learn MoreDonate

  • 1GOALGoal 1: No PovertyGeneral Services, Sramika Dhaathree

  • 3GOALGoal 3: Good Health and Well-beingArogya Dhaathree
  • 4GOALGoal 4: Quality EducationAkshara Dhaathree, Vidya Dhaathree
  • 5GOALGoal 5: Gender EqualityMahila Dhaathree
  • 8GOALGoal 8: Decent Work and Economic GrowthSramika Dhaathree, women’s skill training
  • 10GOALGoal 10: Reduced InequalitiesVikasa Dhaathree
