Aahara Dhaathree

Food Support Wing

Aahara Dhaathree works towards providing food support to needy communities, aiming to reduce hunger and promote food security. The wing organizes food distribution programs for vulnerable groups, supporting children, elderly people, and families in need.

Nutritious food awareness is also encouraged through community activities that promote the value of sharing and compassion, bringing people together through service and care. Aahara Dhaathree believes no one should sleep hungry — its goal is a healthier, more caring society.

Activities & Press Coverage

From our activity record, newest first. Select a page to view it in full.

21 April 2021
Sri Rama Navami food distribution

Municipal Chairperson Smt. Manuja Kiran Reddy and her family served food to destitute people in rural Madanapalle under Aahara Dhaathree.

6 December 2020
Meals after cyclones Nivar and Burevi

Daily-wage workers from Nellore, Kurnool and Guntur living in tents in Madanapalle were given food during the heavy rains.

16 October 2020
World Food Day

Nutrition packs with milk, bread, boiled eggs, fruit and biscuits were distributed in the NG Palli area of Madanapalle.

2020
Thirty days of daily meals

Aahara Dhaathree completed its first 30 days of providing one meal a day to poor and needy people in Madanapalle.

30 August 2020
Launch of Aahara Dhaathree

Aahara Dhaathree was introduced with the motto of providing one meal every day to people in need.

26 August 2020
Mother Teresa birth anniversary

Food, water and fruit were distributed to homeless and needy people in Madanapalle.

31 March 2020
Food for police and truck drivers

Meals were served to police personnel and truck drivers at the Madanapalle–Punganur bypass Y junction during the lockdown.
