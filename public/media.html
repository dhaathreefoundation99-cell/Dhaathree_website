Media & Gallery

A glimpse into the programs, people, and progress that define our work.

Newspaper coverage and photographs of Dhaathree Foundation's programmes in Madanapalle and across Andhra Pradesh. Each report is listed with the publication, the date and a short English summary. Select a clipping to view it in full.

In the News

Reports from February and March 2026.

Radhe Sindhooram — 8 March 2026
“Swathi Chakrapani, a role model in service — District Collector and MLA present award for her service”

At the International Women’s Day celebration in Madanapalle, the MLA, District Collector and District SP presented Dr. Swathi Chakrapani with an award recognising her service, including Ananda Nilayam for the elderly, essential supplies during COVID-19, and free tailoring training and sewing machines for women.

Source: Radhe Sindhooram, print edition.

Nigha Surya — 7 March 2026
“A role model woman in Madanapalle: Dhaathree Foundation founder Swathi Chakrapani”

A Women’s Day profile describing Ananda Nilayam, where elders receive three meals a day and daily care, along with scholarships for poor students and grocery and financial support for needy families.

Source: Nigha Surya, print edition.

Tharanam — 24 February 2026
“Special recognition for Dhaathree Foundation’s services”

Dr. Swathi Chakrapani was honoured as ‘Mahila Samrat’ at the Sir C.V. Raman Ugadi Awards in Vijayawada, in recognition of Dhaathree Foundation’s work including Ananda Nilayam for the elderly and free night tuition centres for poor students.

Source: Tharanam, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: tharanam.net

Surya — 23 February 2026
“Distinguished honour for Dr. Swathi’s selfless service — Sir C.V. Raman Ugadi Award presented”

Dr. Swathi Chakrapani received the Sir C.V. Raman Ugadi Award at Tummalapalli Kalakshetram, Vijayawada, and credited the Dhaathree team for the recognition.

Source: Surya, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: suryaa.com

Andhra Prabha — 22 February 2026
“Distinguished honour for Dr. Swathi’s selfless service”

Ambika Krishna presented the Sir C.V. Raman Academy award to Dr. Swathi Chakrapani for Dhaathree Foundation’s social service in Madanapalle.

Source: Andhra Prabha, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: epaper.prabhanews.com

Nigha Surya — 22 February 2026
“Dhaathree Foundation President Dr. Swathi Chakrapani honoured as ‘Dhaathri Vaibhava Mahila Samrat’”

The Sir C.V. Raman Academy presented the award at its Ugadi Awards in Vijayawada for the Foundation’s service to the elderly, poor students and families in and around Madanapalle.

Source: Nigha Surya, print edition.

Andhra Jyothi — 22 February 2026
“Ugadi award for Swathi Chakrapani”

Dhaathree Foundation founder Swathi Chakrapani received the Ugadi award at the Sir C.V. Raman Ugadi Awards in Vijayawada for her services to the elderly and poor students in Madanapalle.

Source: Andhra Jyothi, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: epaper.andhrajyothy.com

Andhra Prabha — 8 February 2026
“Nutritious food can help cure TB”

Dhaathree Foundation distributed nutrition kits — eggs, bread, groceries and vegetables — to 30 TB patients at the Government Hospital, Madanapalle.

Source: Andhra Prabha, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: epaper.prabhanews.com

Neti Manadesam — 8 February 2026
“Dhaathree distributes nutritious food to TB patients”

With support from Dr. Gumpu Prashanth and Dr. Gumpu Sri Harshini, the Foundation provided a month’s nutrition supplies to 30 TB patients and will support 30 more patients.

Source: Neti Manadesam, print edition. Publisher’s e-paper: netimanadesam.com
