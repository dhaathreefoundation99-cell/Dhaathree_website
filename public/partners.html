Partners & Sponsors

The institutions and organizations that power our community initiatives.

Sponsors

Organisations and individuals who fund Dhaathree Foundation’s programmes.

Programme Collaborators

Organisations we have run programmes with, as recorded in our activity record.

  • SIRDS, Tirupati

    Co-organised scholarships for 80 students (March 2021), the opening of the first Akshara Dhaathree centre (November 2021), menstrual hygiene programmes for girls (2021–22), examination materials for Class X students (April 2022), and flood relief in Kottagudem (July 2022) and Vijayawada (September 2024).

  • Sphoorti Foundation, Hyderabad

    Supported 1,000 health kits for home-quarantined COVID-19 patients (May 2021), seven oxygen concentrators for COVID centres (June 2021), rations for handloom weavers (June 2021) and sweaters for 60 children of migrant workers (January 2021).

  • Pratyusha

    Supported the oxygen concentrators donated to the COVID centres in Madanapalle and B. Kothakota (June 2021).

  • RASS Jana Shikshan Sansthan, Tirupati

    Ran employment training courses for women at the Foundation’s centre from November 2020, with certificates from the Government of India.

  • Sri Padmavathi Mahila University

    Prof. Sujatha and her team trained women in silk cocoon crafts at the Foundation (September 2020).

  • Asha Jyothi Handicapped Welfare Society

    Partnered on free sanitary napkins and health awareness for 600 girls at Gurukul School, Madanapalle (February 2022).

  • Nehru Yuva Kendra, Madanapalle

    Ran the Fit India Freedom Run with Foundation members from Society Colony to NTR Circle (August 2021).

  • CM Reddy Academy

    Co-organised the Sankranti rangoli competition at Srivari Gokulam, Madanapalle (January 2021).
