Akshara Dhaathree

Education & Literacy Wing

Akshara Dhaathree focuses on spreading the light of education among children and communities. It provides learning support, basic education, and skill development opportunities. The wing encourages children to improve their knowledge and creativity, supporting students through guidance, mentoring, and educational activities, with special focus given to underprivileged children and their future growth.

It promotes reading habits, communication skills, and confidence building, working to reduce educational barriers in society and create a positive learning environment for every child. Akshara Dhaathree believes education is the foundation for development — its mission is to empower lives through knowledge.

27 April 2025
Akshara Utsav 2025

Annual celebration of the Akshara Dhaathree study centres at Sri Raja Rajeswari Kalyana Mandapam, Madanapalle, attended by MLA Shajahan Basha.

1 January 2025
New Year at the Shivaji Nagar study centre

MLA Shajahan Basha celebrated the New Year with the children of the Akshara Dhaathree study centre at Shivaji Nagar, Madanapalle.

14 November 2021
Opening of the first Akshara Dhaathree centre

On Children’s Day, study materials were distributed and Akshara Dhaathree was started at YSR Colony, BK Palli, giving children two hours of evening study in their own locality. Organised jointly with SIRDS.

14 November 2020
Children’s Day at Balaji Nagar

Fruits, biscuits and chocolates were distributed to children in Balaji Nagar, Madanapalle. Smt. Kanakamma donated one month of her pension for the event.
