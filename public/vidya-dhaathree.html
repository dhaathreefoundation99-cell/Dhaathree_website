Vidya Dhaathree

Knowledge & Skill Wing

Vidya Dhaathree promotes learning beyond regular education, encouraging knowledge sharing and personal growth. The wing focuses on skills, talents, and practical learning that help individuals discover their abilities and interests.

Workshops and development activities are conducted regularly to support youth in building confidence and skills. Vidya Dhaathree believes knowledge creates empowerment — its aim is to build capable, confident individuals through lifelong learning.

Your donation funds this programme directly in Madanapalle and nearby communities.
