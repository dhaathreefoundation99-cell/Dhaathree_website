General Services

Community Support Wing

General Services provides support for a wide range of social needs, working to bring positive change through service activities. The wing responds to community requirements and challenges as they arise, supporting welfare, awareness, and development programs.

It connects people with opportunities and resources, encouraging volunteer participation and collective responsibility across many areas of public welfare. General Services represents the spirit of helping others — its mission is to serve society with dedication.

Support General Services

Your donation funds this programme directly in Madanapalle and nearby communities.

Activities & Press Coverage

From our activity record, newest first. Select a page to view it in full.

October 2024
Essential commodities for Yanadi families

About 50 Yanadi families near Palamaneru received essential commodities, supported by Sri Shivasankar.

19 September 2024
Flood relief in Vijayawada

Dr. Swathi Chakrapani went door to door in Ogirala village handing over essential commodity kits to 250 flood-hit families, with SIRDS.

28 July 2022
Flood relief in Kottagudem

Families in flood-affected villages near Bhadrachalam each received a rice bag and 18 types of groceries, with SIRDS.

10 December 2021
Clothes stitched by trainees for flood-hit families

About 60 people living in tents in rural Madanapalle received clothes stitched at the Foundation’s training centre.

24 November 2021
Geetavani School students collect aid

Students and staff collected 200 kg of rice, groceries, clothes and ₹3,000 for flood-affected families.

April 2021
Support after a lightning strike

₹5,000 was given to a migrant family from Odisha whose daughter died in a lightning strike, with Sphoorti Foundation.

2 January 2021
Sweaters for children of migrant workers

About 60 children living in tents near Ammacheruvu, Madanapalle, received sweaters, with Sphoorti Foundation.

2 June 2020
Help for a farmer’s family

Essential commodities were given to the family of farmer Eshwaraiah of Jangalapalle, Vampally, who lost three family members in a lightning strike.

2 June 2020
Green fodder for Srivari Gokulam

Two mini trucks of green grass were provided for the cows at Srivari Gokulam near Madanapalle.

4 May 2020
Essentials for the Hijra colony

Around 50 families received rice, essential commodities and vegetables during the lockdown.

29 April 2020
Essentials for migrant workers

Migrant workers from Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh received essential commodities, and masks were given to hospital staff.

6 April 2020
Masks and sanitisers for frontline workers

Masks and sanitisers were supplied to police and municipal sanitation workers in Madanapalle.

2020
Services during the COVID-19 lockdown

Food, groceries and care for homeless people, migrants and frontline workers across Madanapalle during the lockdown.
