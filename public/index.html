About Dhaathree Foundation

Dhaathree Foundation was born from a simple but powerful conviction: every person deserves the chance to flourish. Founded in 2019 and registered under Regd. No. 103/2019, we began our journey in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh.

What Dhaathree means

The word Dhaathree (ధాత్రి) means Earth — the sustainer of all life. Like the earth, we strive to nourish without discrimination.

Our symbol

Cupped hands cradling a world of birds, grain, and open sky — to hold communities gently and help them grow.

Recognised for Our Service

In February 2026, Dhaathree Foundation’s founder Dr. Swathi Chakrapani received the Sir C.V. Raman Ugadi Award in Vijayawada and was honoured as ‘Mahila Samrat’, in recognition of the Foundation’s work with elders and poor students.

In March 2026, the District Collector and MLA presented her with an award at the International Women’s Day celebration in Madanapalle.
