Vikasa Dhaathree

Development Wing

Vikasa Dhaathree works for overall community development, focusing on social, educational, and economic growth. The wing supports initiatives that improve quality of life and encourages participation in sustainable development.

Community-based programs are created for positive change, working toward equal opportunities for everyone. Vikasa Dhaathree believes development should reach all — its vision is inclusive growth and empowerment for stronger, more independent communities.

Activities & Press Coverage

10 July 2025
Education materials for differently abled children

Educational materials and play items were distributed to differently abled students at the Bhavita Centre, Palamaneru.

29 November 2021
Clothes for BC Girls Hostel students

Students living at BC Hostel III, Madanapalle, received clothes donated by Smt. Sirisha Murugesan through the Foundation.

30 August 2021
Fit India Freedom Run

A 2 km run with the Nehru Yuva Kendra team from Society Colony to NTR Circle to encourage daily exercise.

14 January 2021
Sankranti rangoli competition

Organised with CM Reddy Academy at Srivari Gokulam, Madanapalle, with prizes for all participants.

14 June 2020
Support for a differently abled couple

Funds were raised to help a differently abled couple set up a dustless chalk unit. The team also donated blood on World Blood Donor Day.

2 April 2020
Provisions for a specially challenged person

One month of rice and provisions for Nagaraj, who is specially challenged, and for Srinivasulu, who is paralysed, and his family.
