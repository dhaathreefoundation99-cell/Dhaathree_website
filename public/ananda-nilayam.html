Ananda Nilayam

Day Care Centre for the Aged Wing

Ananda Nilayam is dedicated to providing a safe, loving, and dignified shelter for the elderly, homeless, and vulnerable members of our society. We believe that everyone deserves care, respect, and companionship, especially in their twilight years. Our wing focuses on offering comfortable accommodation, healthy nutritious meals, round-the-clock healthcare support, and a peaceful environment where residents can live with dignity and self-respect.

Beyond physical shelter, Ananda Nilayam fosters a warm community where seniors can build friendships, engage in recreational activities, and receive emotional support. Through regular health camps, physical therapy sessions, and spiritual programs, we strive to enrich the lives of our residents, bringing confidence and joy back into their lives.
