Mahila Dhaathree

Women Empowerment Wing

Mahila Dhaathree works for women's empowerment and equality, supporting women through awareness and development programs. The wing encourages education, skills, and independence, promoting confidence and leadership among women.

It supports women in achieving their goals, focusing on dignity, safety, and respect, and creating opportunities for growth and progress. Mahila Dhaathree believes empowered women build stronger communities — its mission is to support every woman's potential.
