About Dhaathree Foundation

The story, mission, and people behind our work in empowering Andhra Pradesh's communities.

Rooted in Seva, Driven by Purpose

Dhaathree Foundation was born from a simple but powerful conviction: every person, regardless of background or circumstance, deserves the chance to flourish. Founded in 2019 and registered under Regd. No. 103/2019, we began our journey in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh.

The word Dhaathree (ధాత్రి) means Earth — the sustainer of all life. Like the earth, we strive to nourish without discrimination, to give without expectation, and to remain steady under the feet of those who need support.

Our symbol — cupped hands cradling a world of birds, grain, and open sky — reflects our mission: to hold communities gently, create space for life to grow, and release potential to soar free.

About the Founder

Dr. Swathi Chakrapani is the founder of Dhaathree Foundation, an organisation dedicated to creating positive social change and empowering communities. Her journey began with a strong vision to serve society and support people in need. With compassion and commitment, she started various initiatives focusing on education, health, women empowerment, and social development. She believes that true service is about creating opportunities and bringing confidence into people's lives.

  • Akshara Dhaathree

    Works towards improving education and learning opportunities for children. Her initiatives encourage students not only to study but also to develop creativity, skills, and personality.

  • Aahara & Arogya Dhaathree

    Supports the importance of nutrition and providing care to those who need it. Her efforts in Arogya Dhaathree focus on health awareness and community well-being.

  • Women Empowerment & Skill Development

    Working actively for women empowerment by providing skill development and self-reliance opportunities. Her vision is to help women become financially independent and confident, promoting vocational training programs that help people learn useful skills for their future.

Dr. Swathi believes that education is the foundation for social transformation. Her service activities reach children, women, senior citizens, and underprivileged communities. She leads with dedication, kindness, and a strong sense of responsibility towards society. Her work reflects the values of humanity, equality, and sustainable development. Through Dhaathree Foundation, she continues to inspire many people to contribute towards a better society. Her mission is to build a community where everyone gets opportunities to grow and succeed. She believes that small acts of service can create big changes in people's lives. Her journey stands as an example of leadership with purpose and compassion. Dr. Swathi continues to work tirelessly to spread hope, education, and empowerment among communities.

Inspiration Behind Dhaathree Foundation

What fuels our commitment and guides our journey to empower communities.

  • Service & Kindness

    The inspiration behind Dhaathree Foundation comes from the belief that humanity grows stronger through kindness, service, and responsibility. The journey is inspired by the idea that every individual deserves respect, care, education, and opportunities to progress. Seeing the needs of children, women, senior citizens, and underprivileged communities created a strong motivation to serve society.

  • Empowerment & Learning

    The organisation was inspired by the thought that education can transform lives and build a better future. The love and concern towards children encouraged the creation of learning opportunities through Akshara Dhaathree. The vision is to support students not only academically but also in creativity, confidence, and personal growth.

  • Care & Opportunity

    The inspiration also comes from the desire to ensure that no person is left behind due to lack of resources. The foundation believes that providing food, health support, and education can bring meaningful change. The idea of Aahara Dhaathree and Arogya Dhaathree was born from the importance of nutrition and well-being. Women empowerment became a key focus to help women gain skills, independence, and self-confidence.

  • Impact & Community

    The organisation draws inspiration from people who dedicate their lives to social welfare and community development. Every smile of a child, every empowered woman, and every supported family becomes a source of encouragement. The foundation believes that true success is measured by the positive impact created in society. It is inspired by the values of compassion, equality, and service without barriers.

The dedication of volunteers and supporters continues to inspire the organisation's mission. The foundation works with the belief that small efforts can create powerful changes. The ultimate inspiration is the dream of a better, educated, healthy, and empowered society where everyone gets a chance to learn, grow, and succeed.
